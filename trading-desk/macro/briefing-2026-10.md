# Macro briefing: October 2026

*Written 15 Oct 2026 from news reports. Prices move daily, so confirm live quotes before acting.*

## What is happening

- **Saudi–Houthi escalation inside the wider Iran war.** Houthi forces have struck Saudi
  targets, including an attack on Riyadh's airport (reported 12 dead, 300+ injured), and Saudi
  officials say Aramco facilities were attacked. A Saudi-led coalition reportedly struck Houthi
  energy terminals and fuel reserves in Yemen.
  ([CNBC, 14 Oct](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/oil-rises-amid-growing-tensions-between-saudis-and-iran-backed-houthis.html),
  [Invezz, 11 Oct](https://invezz.com/ie/news/2026/10/11/brent-crude-oil-price-forecast-as-saudi-arabia-houthi-crisis-escalates/))
- **Strait of Hormuz still constrained.** Kpler data cited by CNBC: ~9.5 mb/d exited Hormuz in
  the latest week, about 30% below pre-war normal.
  ([CNBC, 13 Oct](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/13/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz.html))
- **Saudi Arabia says exports will keep flowing**, which has capped spikes.
  ([OilPrice.com](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Oil-Prices-Retreat-as-Saudi-Arabia-Pledges-to-Keep-Exports-Flowing.html))
- **IEA (Oct report)** warned diesel-market constraints could last well into 2027.

## What this means: scenarios, not predictions

| Scenario | Brent | Likely losers | Likely winners | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Escalation**: Aramco output hit, Hormuz flows fall further | $115+ | OMCs, aviation, paints, tyres, autos, banks; INR weakens | ONGC, OIL, defence, IT, gold | Respect stops on trading positions; don't buy the first dip in hurt sectors |
| **Grinding stalemate** (current) | $100–110 | Same names drift lower; volatile | Refiners (diesel cracks), IT | Small tranches only; build a watchlist of quality names in hurt sectors |
| **De-escalation**: ceasefire, Hormuz normalises | < $90 | Upstream, defence give back gains | OMCs, IndiGo, paints, tyres, autos rally sharply | The big opportunity. Have entry levels ready *before* it happens |

## Open questions to track weekly
