## Target allocation (long-term money)

| Asset | Target % | Vehicle examples (direct plans) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large cap / index | 40% | Nifty 50 or Nifty Next 50 index fund | Lowest cost, hard to beat |

| Mid + small cap | 15% | One mid-cap, optionally one small-cap | Only for 7+ year money; deep drawdowns |
| International | 5–10% | US/global index FoF (check RBI overseas limits) | Diversifies away from INR/oil risk |
| Gold | 5–10% | Gold ETF / gold fund / SGB if available | Hedge for war/oil/INR shocks, as in 2026 |
| Debt | 10–15% | Short-duration / corporate bond / arbitrage | Stability + dry powder for dips |

Rules:
- **Direct plans, not regular**: lower expense ratio, same fund.
- Max 5–6 funds total. More funds ≠ more diversification; check portfolio overlap.
- **SIPs never stop because of news.** A war-driven fall means SIPs buy more units.
- Extra cash in a crash: deploy through a 3–6 month STP from a liquid fund, not lump sum.
- Rebalance once a year, or when any asset drifts > 5 points from target.
- Judge funds on 5-year rolling returns vs their benchmark, not last year's return.
